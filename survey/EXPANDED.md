# Expanded benchmark checkpoint

Work stopped at the requested checkpoint. Six multi-object cases have complete source, detailed
artifact, prompt-only reconstruction, and exact prompt evidence: astronaut crew, food table,
Amsterdam market, train platform, workspace, and dogs on a beach. Four additional licensed CC0
sources are encoded but not generated: kitchen table, minimal interior, mountain hikers, and
street bicycles.

Open [`../survey_expannded.html`](../survey_expannded.html) for the completed visual pairs. This is
an evidence checkpoint, not a finished ten-case statistical report. Early review suggests that
object inventory and broad layout survive better than exact human identity, small text, logos,
counts, and fine object geometry. Those should be the next codec optimization targets.

All expanded sources came from Wikimedia Commons records marked CC0 or public domain. Benchmark
copies were resized to at most 1920 pixels on the longest edge for tractable local processing.
